Three commits landed on `dev` this week. One is pure UI polish on the homepage hero, and two make the Signals feed more honest about severity. The biggest one also opens a new research track on abliterated and "uncensored" open-weight models.

## The hero eye blinks now

The ASCII eye on the homepage blinks in place ([bb1464c](https://github.com/offsitedarklabs/offsitedark/commit/bb1464ca04e1fb3ce7bf97b47e95f302bc901864)). Every animation frame shares one geometry, so the scaler only swaps glyphs and never re-lays out the block. That keeps the hero from jumping or reflowing mid-blink. Opening is the close played in reverse, and the wings splay on their own frames, separate from the lids.

The frames are generated, not hand-drawn. `scripts/generate-hero-blink.py` writes 27 frames to `public/ascii/hero-blink/` (blink stages `b0` to `b8` × wing states `w0` to `w2`) plus a `manifest.json`. A new `ascii-blink.tsx` component plays them back, and `ascii-block.tsx` and `ascii-scaler.tsx` were updated to handle frame swaps.

## Signals: a severity ledger that tells the truth

Two problems with Signals: every score mark rendered red, and a CISA KEV mention counted as "listed" even when the row said *not* listed. [c3574c5](https://github.com/offsitedarklabs/offsitedark/commit/c3574c5dbd7d42401819c728be1f9a68e3008077) fixes both. It adds a severity ledger with a key, plus blue bands for catalog status, so KEV status reads correctly at a glance. Most of the change is in `globals.css` (~370 new lines).

The same commit reworked a large set of older Signals entries to match their advisories and added new ones. Among them: a late-2026 CISA KEV cluster, IngressNightmare (CVE-2025-1974), an OpenSSH/musl auth bypass, a Linux IPv4 TCP use-after-free, a GitLab regex-compiler RCE, and the GoAhead TLS issues across OpenSSL/LibreSSL/mbedTLS.

A follow-up in [d43b18b](https://github.com/offsitedarklabs/offsitedark/commit/d43b18b840dcfbdc7a17b7bf3954f505a51e0832) finished the job without touching the parser. CVSS lines in 17 news entries were reworded so the existing severity parser picks them up and renders the right critical/high/medium pills. Where sources disagree, both scores are kept (e.g. GitLab WebAuthn: CVSS 3.1 6.8 Medium, NVD 8.1 High).

## New research track: abliterated models

The rest of [d43b18b](https://github.com/offsitedarklabs/offsitedark/commit/d43b18b840dcfbdc7a17b7bf3954f505a51e0832) is new content on open-weight LLMs with their refusals removed ("abliterated", "obliterated", "heretic" builds):

- **Three essays** in a new `ai` research category: one on DeepSeek obliterated/abliterated models, one on the wider obliterated/heretic/uncensored open-weight landscape, and one on how uncontrolled abliterated LLMs fail.
- **13 model dossiers** under a new `abliterated` grep category. They cover DeepSeek R1 distills and V3/V4.1-flash variants, Dolphin Mistral 24B Venice Edition, Gemma 4 E4B, GLM 4.7 Flash, gpt-oss-20b, Lexi Uncensored v2 (Llama 3.1 8B), mlabonne's abliterated Llama 3.1 8B Instruct, and Qwen 3.8 27B.

To make all this findable, the homepage hero now opens with *"A public archive of cybersecurity research."* The AGENTIC SECURITY section links straight to **AI research** (`/research?category=ai`) and **Model greps** (`/greps`). The research filter gains `ai`, and the greps page gets an "Abliterated" filter and a link back to the essays.
