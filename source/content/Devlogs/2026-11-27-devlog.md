## UI: sticky header and phones

The header is sticky now ([e48f67e](https://github.com/offsitedarklabs/offsitedark/commit/e48f67e20a55ad842564eafce9898bb1f129b2f2)). The new `nav-scroll.tsx` compacts it once you scroll past 12 px and expands it again when you scroll up within 100 px of the top. It's direction-aware with hysteresis, uses a passive listener, is SSR safe, and keeps a fixed-height placeholder so content never jumps. The header is solid and opaque with a 1 px red edge, and `scroll-padding-top` follows `--header-h` so anchor links don't land under it. Nav links are about 35% larger.

The phone-portrait pass removes horizontal overflow at 360 px across the ledgers, research, forum, nodes, and tools pages. Tap targets are 36 px, the FEATURED label stops overlapping the meta line, badge and label pairs don't split across lines, and long CVE titles get the full width above their severity pills.

## Hero ASCII

The wings loop continuously now at a 240 ms step, choppy on purpose. `generate-hero-blink.py` shifts the whole wing silhouette per frame so the tips move with the body, and `ascii-block.tsx` pins the barcode rows and the dotted rule so they never animate. Two new modules draw the right banner: `ascii-wordmark.ts` lays a faint OFFSITEDARK wordmark across it and `ascii-noise.ts` adds deterministic seeded noise. There's no `Math.random` in there, so server and client render the same thing.

The same commit made the connected banner much bigger. That didn't hold up. The text crowded and spilled out of the red band, so [624d4c6](https://github.com/offsitedarklabs/offsitedark/commit/624d4c652b0f1aa2e46775b328a1bb792d4c8d14) puts the original smaller banner back and drops the oversized CSS. The glitch and flicker animations stay.

## Code review fixes

- Fixed the `/about` hydration mismatch and every react-hooks lint error in `ascii-scaler.tsx` and `ascii-blink.tsx` (`useSyncExternalStore` for media queries, derived state, refs in effects).
- YAML dates are normalized to ISO strings and formatted in UTC. That fixes invalid `<time datetime>` attributes and off-by-one month groups outside UTC.
- New `src/lib/metadata.ts` gives every route canonical, Open Graph, and Twitter metadata. `research/[slug]` only resolves research posts now, so news titles don't leak in.
- `feed.xml` gets proper XML and CDATA escaping, permalink GUIDs, date validation, and error handling.
- Site search closes on outside pointerdown and blur. The ledgers respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and stop double-scrolling under StrictMode.

`tsc` and `eslint` are clean (0 errors, 0 warnings), and the production build renders 324/324 static pages. The sticky header feel and wordmark legibility still need a look in a real browser after deploy.
